WELCOME TO HAUT GAMES: The Disaperance of Leslie Blake

The vision of this game is meant to function like an escape room in a box/cold case files games that you can find nowadays, but on your computer as an interactive story. Click around, discover information, clues, and ultimately solve the case… Are you up for the task? Description: Your friend, Leslie Blake, is missing. The police have no suspects, no leads, and frankly don’t think there is much to do. They say with Leslie’s history and all the evidence that they gathered, they believe that she just ran away. You, however, think there is something else afoot. Leslie was one of your closest friends from college and something felt off about this. Leslie was not the most open about her past, but no matter what the police say about her “history”, the Leslie you know now was not someone who would just leave out of the blue. She was well-loved by her friends, had a boyfriend, and she was in the middle of a semester at college. She had plans for her future. Most importantly, she wouldn’t have left without taking with her her most prized possession: the letters her mom had written her before she passed. You couldn’t just sit there and do nothing. You needed to find Leslie Blake.
