MISSING PERSON CASE

CASE FILE: LESLIE BLAKE

Status: Missing
Police Conclusion: Voluntary Disappearance
Your Conclusion: Unknown

Case Summary

Your friend, Leslie Blake, is missing.

The police have no suspects, no leads, and frankly don't think there is much to do. They believe that Leslie simply ran away.

You disagree.

Leslie was one of your closest friends from college. She had plans, people who cared about her, and a future she was actively building. Most importantly, she would never have left behind her most prized possession: the letters her mother wrote before she passed away.

The police may have stopped looking, but you haven't.

Sitting before you is the first collection of evidence. Somewhere within it is the truth.
